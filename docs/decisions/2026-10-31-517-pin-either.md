- **III.1 Por.'s axioms pinned to either axiom while #517 lands, and Jev's glossary told of
  the move (tooling, [#517](https://github.com/dodecaplex-co/stoicheia/issues/517)).** The
  owner approved, on 30 October 2026, Metric 9 (`Tacit.area_congruent`) and Common Notion 4
  for figures (`Tacit.figures_coincide`) both on main briefly, from the part of #517 that
  adds the new axiom until the part that removes Metric 9, which goes before this release.
  Those are the coordinator's words for the owner's choice, not the owner's own. The owner's
  choice of 28 October 2026, that I.4's area clause rest on the new axiom in Metric 9's
  place, is recorded in [its entry](2026-10-28-common-notion-4-checks.md). The coordinator's
  call, on 30 October, is that the certificate's expectations accept either axiom for the
  pinned entries while both stand, so that each part of the move passes on its own, and that
  the move's last part pins the new axiom only. This part lands first, before any Lean
  changes.

  The pin. The first expectation held III.1 Por.'s `axioms` to exactly one list, 37 tacit
  rules with Metric 9 among them. It becomes an expectation with `one_of`, the form I.4's
  states already use, told apart by `axioms`. The state "Metric 9" is main's expectation
  word for word: the same list, the same exclusion of `Tacit.centre_unique` and the same
  count. The state "Common Notion 4 for figures" asks for the same list with
  `Tacit.figures_coincide` in the place of `Tacit.area_congruent`, which is the value the
  built branch of #517 reached, with the same exclusion and count. Each state pins an exact
  list, as main did; the expectation now accepts the second list as well. The two lists
  differ, so at most one state holds, and a porism that rests on both axioms, or on neither,
  holds neither state and fails, naming each state with what sank it. On main the porism is
  in the state "Metric 9". The file's `about` now says that III.1 Por. is held this way too.
  No code changes with it: `check_expectations` in `scripts/check_fidelity.py` already reads
  `one_of`.

  The glossary. Jev's question generator checks that each word of its glossary is defined
  in the file its group names, and refuses a word found elsewhere ("glossary registry
  moved"). The group `Tacit/Superposition` holds one word, `primitives`, which #517 moves to
  a file of its own, `Elements/Foundations/Superposition/Primitives.lean`, shared by the rule
  of superposition and the new axiom. A new table, `REGISTRY_FILES`, lets that one group be
  found in `Elements/Foundations/Tacit/Superposition.lean`, where it is on main, or in the
  new file. Every other group is found in its own file alone, as before, and a word in any
  other file is still refused. No check of `checks/` runs the glossary, since its tests were
  taken out ([that entry](2026-10-30-jev-question-tests-out.md)); the change keeps the
  generator usable through the move.

  This entry approves the change to `tests/certificate_expectations.json`: III.1 Por.'s pin
  on its axioms in two states, as described above.

  The change to `scripts/audit/jev_questions.py`, the table `REGISTRY_FILES` and the
  glossary's reading of it, is approved here as well.

  The contract, the last tooling part of #517, puts the pin back to one list, the new
  axiom's, and the group back to one file, the new one.

  Why: #517 lands in parts, each green alone. Today the pin holds III.1 Por. to Metric 9
  exactly, so the part that moves I.4 to the new axiom would fail it; and the glossary would
  refuse `primitives` once the part that adds the axiom moves it. Those parts belong to
  other suppliers, and the expectations and the generator to the release-tooling cell, so
  the pin and the glossary are widened first, by their owner, and narrowed again once the
  move is done.
